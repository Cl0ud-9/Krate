# Code of Conduct

Krate is a small project run by one person, and anyone taking part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere
else the project lives) is expected to help keep it a friendly place to be.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, including with people who are new to this.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Assume good intent.
- Keep feedback specific and useful: what's wrong, and what would be better.
- Respect that the maintainer has the final say on what goes into Krate, and that "no" is sometimes the answer.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour.
- Sharing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, including low-effort or machine-generated issues and pull requests posted in bulk.
- Asking for, or posting, ways to get around the licenses of Krate or of the apps in its catalog.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block anyone who doesn't follow this
code, temporarily or permanently. To report a problem privately, use the contact details in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Reports are kept confidential.
